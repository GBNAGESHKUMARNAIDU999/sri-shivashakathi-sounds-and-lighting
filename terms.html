1. Agreement to Terms

These Terms and Conditions constitute a legally binding agreement between the client ("Client", "You") and Sri Shivashakti Sound, Lighting & Event Management ("we", "our", or "us"). By booking our sound equipment, stage lighting, mandap setups, or event production services, you agree to comply with these terms.

2. Booking Confirmation & Payment Schedule

To reserve dates and guarantee equipment availability for weddings, parties, or festivals:

  • Advance Booking Deposit: A mutually agreed advance token (typically 30% to 50%) is required upon booking confirmation to secure the event date and schedule technical personnel.
  • Balance Payment: The remaining balance must be settled on the date of the event prior to completion or upon successful handover of the sound and lighting production.
  • Direct Producer Pricing: All quotes provided reflect direct service rates without middleman surcharges. Custom requirements requested on-site outside the agreed package will be billed as additional units.

3. Venue Access & Power Requirements

Smooth audio-visual execution depends on timely venue readiness:

  • Early Access: Our crew must be granted access to the premises at least 4 to 8 hours prior to the event (or earlier for multi-floor facade lighting) for rigging, sound tuning, and calibration.
  • Power Infrastructure: The Client must ensure adequate electrical power supply (Single Phase or Three Phase as specified) or arrange backup generator (DG) facilities. Sri Shivashakti is not liable for interruptions resulting from main municipal grid failure unless hired with dedicated generator backup.
  • Structural Rigging: Facade lights, serial light cascades, and truss assemblies require secure anchor points. Our team adheres strictly to certified electrical safety and load-balancing protocols.

4. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

We understand event dates can shift due to unforeseen circumstances:

  • Rescheduling: Requests made at least 7 days in advance of the event date will be accommodated without forfeiture of the deposit, subject to equipment calendar availability.
  • Cancellations: In the event of Client cancellation within 48 hours of setup commencement, advance deposits may be withheld to cover incurred transport logistics, labor allocations, and reserved gear.

5. Equipment Safety & Care

Our heavy-duty speakers, Ahuja amplifiers, moving head beams, and waterproof LED fixtures are commercial-grade. The Client agrees to provide reasonable site security to prevent intentional vandalism, unauthorized tampering by unauthorized guests, or liquid spills directly into acoustic consoles.

6. Noise Regulations & Permissions

The Client is responsible for obtaining any necessary local municipal, police, or resident association sound permissions required for late-night outdoor celebrations. Sri Shivashakti technicians will comply with decibel guidelines mandated by local authorities.
