Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I book for my event?

We recommend booking at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance for weddings and large building decorations. For urgent bookings, call us directly at 9902138349 for instant availability.

Do you provide electrical backup & safety?

Yes, all our LED fixtures and sound setups are tested with waterproof heavy-duty controllers, circuit breakers, and certified wiring configurations.

Can we customize the light colors & running patterns?

Absolutely. We provide customized color themes (golden amber, royal blue, emerald green, rainbow cascade) tailored to your building or stage architecture.

Do you serve outside Bangalore?

Yes! We travel across Bangalore Urban, Bangalore Rural, Chikkaballapur, Tumkur, and nearby districts for wedding and event management assignments.
