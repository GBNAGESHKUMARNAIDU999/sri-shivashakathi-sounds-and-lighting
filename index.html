About Us

Welcome to Sri Shivashakti

Sri Shivashakti is a premier Bangalore-based lighting, acoustic sound, and full-scale event management production specialist. We bring magical atmospheres to building decorations, weddings, birthdays, temple festivals, and corporate celebrations.

Equipped with high-lumen Flood Lights, Tape Lights, LED Running Sets, Poke Lights, Par Cans, Moving Heads, Multicolor Serial Lights, and Concert-Grade Professional Sound Systems — all at competitive and affordable pricing.

"From Intimate Gatherings To Grand Festivities, We Bring Light, Sound & Lifelong Memories Together."
