Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Sri Shivashakti Bangalore

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sri Shivashakti Sound, Lighting & Event Management ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide premier 3D sound systems, high-lumen architectural LED lighting, stage decoration, and end-to-end event production across Bangalore and surrounding regions.

We respect your personal privacy and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of any information you share when requesting event quotations, consulting with our lighting specialists, or booking our services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you to furnish accurate event production quotes and deliver safe on-site execution:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact, and email address.
  • Event Specifics: Celebration type (wedding, birthday, temple festival, reception, corporate event), expected dates, duration, and venue location in Bangalore.
  • Technical Requirements: Power infrastructure notes, indoor/outdoor preferences, line array audio requirements, and structural facade heights.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your details strictly for professional event production and coordination:

  • To prepare customized, transparent event packages and written cost estimates without hidden markups.
  • To coordinate pre-event site visits, electrical safety checks, and high-rise rigging logistics.
  • To communicate through direct phone calls, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your event setup schedule.
  • To provide emergency on-site technical support and follow-up service quality assessments.

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your contact numbers or personal information with third-party advertisers or telemarketers.

4. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

When you initiate contact using our website WhatsApp links or phone buttons, your chat messages are handled securely through the official WhatsApp application under WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protocols.

5. Cookies & Website Performance

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies and browser storage (such as saving your cookie consent preferences and local caching) to ensure high-speed media streaming, smooth 3D carousel animations, and responsive interactions. No invasive tracking or behavioral profiling cookies are used.

6. Media & Event Photography

Images and videos displayed in our portfolio showcase public celebrations, facade illuminations, and stage rigging completed by our certified technicians. If you are a client or property owner and wish to request the modification or removal of any media featuring your private property, please contact us and we will promptly accommodate your request.
